Privacy policy

Effective 22 October 2026 · getsantulan.in

In short: everything you enter in Santulan stays on your own phone or browser. We don't run accounts, we don't collect your meals or answers, and there are no ads or trackers.

What Santulan stores

When you use the app, it saves the following in your browser's local storage on your device:

  • Your setup answers: name, age range, sex (optional), approximate weight, goal, sleep, eating and activity habits, diet type and veg days.
  • The meals you log, the days you mark as skipped, glasses of water, your favourite meals and your streak history.

This data is used only inside the app, to set your daily targets and suggest meals. It is never sent to us or to anyone else.

What we don't do

  • No sign-up, no account and no server-side copy of your data.
  • No analytics, advertising or tracking tools.
  • No selling or sharing of personal data.

Third-party services

The app loads its typefaces from Google Fonts. Like any website request, this lets Google see your IP address and browser details. See Google's privacy policy for how it handles this. The site is hosted on Netlify, which keeps standard server logs.

Voice input

If you tap the microphone to speak a meal, your browser's built-in speech service turns your voice into text. On Chrome this is provided by Google and may be processed on Google's servers. Santulan only receives the text, and only while you are speaking. You can always type instead.

Report cards and sharing

The weekly report card is drawn on your phone. It is only saved or shared when you tap Download or Share, and it goes wherever you choose to send it.

Backups

"Download backup" saves a file of your data to your device. That file is yours: we never receive it. Keep it somewhere safe if it matters to you.

Deleting your data

Because the data lives on your device, you control it. To delete it, remove Santulan from your home screen and clear this site's data in your browser settings. Once cleared, it cannot be recovered unless you kept a backup.

Health information

Santulan gives general wellness guidance based on typical food values. It is not medical advice and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Speak to a doctor or registered dietitian about your own health.

Age

Santulan is meant for adults aged 18 and over.

Changes

If this policy changes, for example when optional sign-in or sync is added, we'll update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.
