# Santulan

A meal tracker for Indian food. Log meals the way you'd say them ("2 roti, dal aur thoda chawal") and Santulan works out what you ate and suggests what to eat next to fill protein, B12, iron and fibre gaps.

**Live:** https://getsantulan.in

- 225 common North Indian dishes with Hinglish aliases
- Personal setup: targets and tips based on your goal, diet, activity and habits
- Works offline and installs to your home screen
- Your data stays on your phone. No accounts, no tracking. See the [privacy policy](privacy.html).

Nutrition values are approximate, for general wellness, not medical advice.
